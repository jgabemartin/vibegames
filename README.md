# vibegames
repo for vibe-coded games

## Games

- [platformer-game-v1.5](platformer-game-v1.5/): **SkidRoller v1.5**, a browser BMX platformer with three levels (Day, Sunset and Night), each ending in a boss fight, plus five weather zones, SuperHop double jumps, music and sound effects. See [its README](platformer-game-v1.5/README.md) for controls and details.
- [batting-practice-v1.9](batting-practice-v1.9/): **Batting Practice v1.9**, a top-down baseball hitting game. Time your swing with Space to place hits away from seven fielders. Each round is 20 pitches, and points come from shaded zones: holes in the infield, outfield hits, gappers, down the line, off the wall and home runs. Opens with a ballpark organ intro and has a recorded bat crack plus sound effects. See [its README](batting-practice-v1.9/README.md) for controls and details.
- [skyraider-v1.1](skyraider-v1.1/): **SkyRaider v1.1**, a World War I biplane bombing run with 16-bit-style pixel art. Fly over farmland, trenches and army camps and bomb military targets in 60 seconds with 2 planes. Bombs keep the plane's speed and drift in the wind, and higher drops score up to double. A bombsight helps you aim. Spare the villages and dodge flak shells from barrage blimps and anti-aircraft guns: a plane survives one hit. Recorded sounds include the engine, bomb whistle, explosions, gunfire, air-raid sirens over the villages and an alarm at each enemy base. See [its README](skyraider-v1.1/README.md) for controls and details.

## How to play

These games need no install and no internet connection.

1. Download [vibegames-main.zip](https://github.com/jgabemartin/vibegames/archive/refs/heads/main.zip). You can also click the green **Code** button on the repo page, then **Download ZIP**.
2. Unzip it.
3. Open the game's folder (for example, `platformer-game-v1.5`) and double-click `index.html`. It opens in your browser.
